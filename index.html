FAQ

Good to know.

What is a workflow?

A workflow starts when something happens in a connected app, such as a new YouTube upload. EMERQE then carries out the steps you’ve defined, such as writing a recap and saving a draft.

Which apps can I connect?

EMERQE is designed for apps such as Gmail, YouTube, Instagram, and Outlook. The available triggers and actions depend on the app.

Can I review what AI writes?

Yes. The example workflow saves a Gmail draft so you can read and edit it before sending.

What happens when I run the demo?

The demo simulates the workflow in your browser using sample data. It requires no account and sends no messages.
